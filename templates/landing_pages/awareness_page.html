What Just Happened?

You received a test email that mimicked a real phishing attack. By clicking the link and/or entering information, you demonstrated how easy it can be to fall victim to these types of attacks. Real phishing attacks can lead to:

  • Stolen credentials and passwords
  • Compromised company data
  • Malware infections
  • Financial loss
  • Identity theft

🚩 Red Flags You Should Look For:

  • Urgent or threatening language - "Your account will be suspended!" or "Act now!"
  • Unexpected requests - Unsolicited password resets or verification requests
  • Suspicious sender addresses - Look carefully at the email domain
  • Generic greetings - "Dear Customer" instead of your actual name
  • Poor grammar and spelling - Professional organizations proofread their emails
  • Suspicious links - Hover over links to see the real destination before clicking
  • Unexpected attachments - Don't open attachments from unknown senders

What Should You Do?

If you encounter a suspicious email in the future:

  1. Don't click links or download attachments from suspicious emails
  2. Verify the sender by contacting them through official channels
  3. Check the URL by hovering over links before clicking
  4. Report it to your IT security team immediately
  5. Delete the email after reporting it
